# Aarsopgoerelse

This project is supposed to be a standalone program for windows, linux and mac, that can generate a good estimate of how much any person has paid too much or too little in danish income tax based on information, that the person can provide to the program.

At first it targets unmarried people who don't own their own home or company, and don't have income from companies abroad. The target is at first people who don't earn more than about 600k DKK yearly, and who have more than 15 years left on the job market.
